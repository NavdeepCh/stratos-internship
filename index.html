Frequently asked questions

How do I select a track?

Click a track card. The checkout dialog opens with that track selected, and you can review or complete the form.

When is an order saved?

The intended server flow validates the submitted details and writes an order with Pending payment status to Firestore before creating a payment session.

Are the internship and certificate free?

Please check the current terms shown at checkout. Any price, eligibility, task-completion, and certificate conditions should be clearly disclosed before you proceed.
